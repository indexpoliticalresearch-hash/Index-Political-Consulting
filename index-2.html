Who We Are

Insight that starts with people.

Index Political Consulting is a research and consulting initiative focused on public opinion, field research, outreach and strategic communication.

We believe responsible decisions begin by listening carefully, collecting information thoughtfully and presenting findings clearly. Our approach puts people, accuracy and practical action at the centre.
